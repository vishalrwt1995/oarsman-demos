AI and call recording

How Oarsman Front Desk tells callers about AI and recording, and the rules built into every assistant we set up.

Callers are told up front

Every call starts with a line like: "Hi, you've reached [Salon]. I'm the virtual assistant, and this call is recorded." The business approves the exact wording.

Recording

Calls are recorded and transcribed so bookings can be checked and mistakes fixed. Some US states, including California, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Montana, New Hampshire, Pennsylvania and Washington, require every party to consent to recording. That's one reason the notice comes at the very start of the call.

Texts and outbound calls

  • We don't make outbound AI calls.
  • Missed-call texts go only to people who called the business, and they can reply STOP to opt out.

Who processes the call

Calls run through third-party providers: an AI voice platform (such as Retell or Vapi), the AI model and speech providers it uses, a telephony provider, and the calendar or booking tool. Each one handles data under its own terms. See our privacy policy.

What it won't do

The assistant isn't for emergencies. It sticks to bookings and the business's approved answers, and when it isn't sure, it hands the call to a person.
