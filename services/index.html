Oarsman Front Desk

Monthly subscription from $149. See pricing.

What you get

  • An AI assistant answering every call, 24/7
  • Answers to your FAQs
  • Booking and rescheduling in your calendar or booking tool
  • A booking link texted to missed callers
  • A hand-off note to your staff
  • A weekly call report
  • Setup, testing and maintenance by us

Good to know

  • Every call opens by saying it's a virtual assistant and that the call is recorded.
  • We don't make outbound AI calls. Texts only go to people who called you.
  • Not tested on a real phone line yet. We test on a real number before you go live.
