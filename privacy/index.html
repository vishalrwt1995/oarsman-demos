Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026. In plain English: what happens to information on this website and in our service.

Who we are

Oarsman AI is a small studio run by an individual sole proprietor based in India. It is not a registered company. "We" and "us" here mean Oarsman AI.

This website

  • The site is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use cookies, analytics, ads or tracking scripts.
  • GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and secure its hosting. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • The demos run in your own browser. Files you load into the CSV dashboard are never uploaded. The SaaS demo keeps its sample data in your browser's local storage, which you can clear at any time. The receptionist demo doesn't make calls or send anything.

When you contact us

If you email us or message us on Upwork, we use what you send (your name, contact details and message) only to reply and to prepare a quote or proposal. We don't sell it or use it for unrelated marketing. We keep it while we're talking or working together, plus a reasonable period for our records, and delete it on request unless the law requires us to keep it.

When our AI receptionist is live for a business

  • AI is used. An AI voice agent answers calls, and AI models are used to understand callers and summarise calls.
  • Calls are recorded and processed by third parties. Calls and texts may be recorded, transcribed and processed by third-party providers: the AI voice platform (such as Retell or Vapi), the AI model and speech providers it uses, telephony and SMS providers, the business's calendar or booking tool, and our automation host. Each one handles data under its own terms.
  • What we keep. Caller name, number, what they asked for, booking details, and call recordings and transcripts, kept only to provide the service to that business and to fix problems. We don't sell caller data or use it for our own marketing.
  • The business's role. The business decides what the assistant asks for and how long records are kept, and we act on its instructions. Callers are told at the start of every call that it's a virtual assistant and the call is recorded. See AI and call recording.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. If you called one of our clients, you can contact that business, or us. For anything we hold, use the details on our contact page.

Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes and show the date at the top.
