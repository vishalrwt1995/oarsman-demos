About Oarsman AI

The oarsman does the rowing so you can steer. We take the phone off your hands so you can focus on the client in your chair.

Who we are

Oarsman AI is a small, new studio run by an individual sole proprietor based in India. We build and run AI receptionists for appointment businesses, starting with salons, lash and beauty studios and day spas in the US, then the UK.

What we believe

  • Honest labels. Our demos say what's real, what's a sample and what's simulated. We don't show client logos, reviews or results we don't have.
  • Tested work. We publish test numbers, not promises.
  • Clear AI use. Callers are always told they're talking to a virtual assistant. AI tools also help us write code and copy, and we check the results.

Working across time zones

We're in India (IST). Email support replies by the next business day. Fixes often happen while you sleep, and the weekly report keeps you up to date.
